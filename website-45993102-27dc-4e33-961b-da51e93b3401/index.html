Privacy

Your resume is yours.

We wrote this in plain English, not legalese. If anything here is unclear, email us and we'll fix it.

What we process

When you upload a resume, we read its text and structure to compute a score and category breakdown. If you paste a job description, we read that too. The analysis happens in your session.

What we store

We do not store the original file by default. If you choose to save a version to your history, we store a snapshot of the parsed content and the analysis — not the original PDF/DOCX. Account data (email) is stored only if you create an account.

Deletion

You can delete any saved version from the History page in one click. Deletion is permanent and cannot be undone. Deleting your account removes all associated versions and analyses.

Retention

Unsaved analyses are discarded at the end of your session. Saved versions are kept until you delete them. We do not retain inactive accounts beyond 24 months — you'll get an email reminder before any cleanup.

What we don't do

  • ✕ We do not sell or share your resume content with third parties.
  • ✕ We do not use your resume to train AI models.
  • ✕ We do not auto-apply to jobs on your behalf.
  • ✕ We do not make unverifiable security claims. We follow standard practices and will publish a security overview when our audit completes.

Honest disclaimer

ResumeLift gives you a heuristic estimate based on common recruiter and ATS behavior. A higher score does not guarantee an interview or a job — it means your resume is more likely to be parsed and read. Use it as a second opinion, not a verdict.
